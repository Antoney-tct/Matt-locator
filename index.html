How it works

Four steps, no waiting at the stage guessing.

1

Search a route

Tell us where you're starting and where you're headed — we show every sacco running that road.

2

Compare vehicles

Fares, seats left, and departure estimates side by side, so you're not choosing blind.

3

Watch it approach

A live map simulation shows the vehicle moving through stages toward you.

4

Board with a ticket

Pick a seat, pay by simulated M-Pesa, and get a QR ticket — no cash needed at the door.

This is a class prototype built for BSD 227 – Internet Programming I. Vehicle positions are simulated, and M-Pesa payment is a simulation — no real transport or money moves through this page.
