A clearer way to move

Built around the small decisions that make a journey feel easier.

Matatu Tracker brings route search, vehicle visibility, seat booking, and digital tickets into one simple flow for Nairobi commuters.

Why it exists

Less guessing at the stage. More confidence on the road.

Public transport is full of useful information, but it is often scattered between stage signs, conversations, and waiting. This prototype imagines what happens when the essentials are collected in one place.

Search a corridor, compare the available vehicles, see a simulated approach on the map, and reserve a seat before you leave home.

01
